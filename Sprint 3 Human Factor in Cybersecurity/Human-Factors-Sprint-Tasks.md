# Human Factors Sprint — Task 2

**CyBlack September '26 Internship**
**Task:** Develop a Custom Security Awareness Program/Campaign using VIVIDA Studio

> The challenge is not simply to generate AI content. Your team should demonstrate that you understand the cybersecurity risks affecting people within your chosen industry and can communicate those risks in a way that is clear, relevant and engaging.

---

## Objective

Explore how cybersecurity awareness can be made more effective by combining:

- Human-risk identification
- Industry and organisational context
- Cybersecurity awareness principles
- Creative communication
- AI-enabled content creation
- Team collaboration

By the end of the sprint, your team should have created a **consistent cybersecurity awareness campaign** that reflects realistic human-factor risks within your organisation.

You should also be able to explain the reasoning behind your choices:

- What risks you identified
- Who your content is aimed at
- Why you chose particular scenarios
- How your campaign could influence safer behaviour

---

## Task Steps (from the VIVIDA Creative Brief)

The sprint runs in four steps, all built around one fictional organisation ("one world").

### Step 1 — Create your world

Invent a fictional organisation and build its identity in VIVIDA Studio. Define its:

1. Name
2. Industry
3. Personality
4. Visual style
5. Characters
6. Environments

> ⚠️ Realistic-styled worlds may hit limitations with video creation, so use discretion.

There are no predefined organisations, themes or visual styles. Your group has full freedom to experiment.

### Step 2 — Content challenge

Produce the campaign assets (see **Deliverables** below). Every piece must tackle the human side of cybersecurity.

### Step 3 — Creative tips

- **Brand consistency:** All assets should feel like they belong to the same organisation.
- **Visual quality:** Use Studio's image and video tools to produce professional, engaging content.
- **Messaging:** Keep text concise, accessible and relevant to everyday employee behaviour.
- **Experimentation:** Explore camera angles, environments, characters and styles while staying consistent.

> ⚠️ VIVIDA's AI is great, but your judgement matters. Check the output and edit it.

### Step 4 — Showcase

Each group presents its organisation and the content it created:

1. Share your creative vision
2. Explain your decisions
3. Highlight discoveries and challenges you met using Studio

## Deliverables

| Qty | Asset | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Image posts | Unique visuals with messages relevant to your organisation |
| 6 | Text posts | Short written posts carrying your organisation's security messages |
| 2 | Video reels | 6–10 seconds max; short videos that bring your organisation to life |
| 1 | Graphic | A branded infographic, poster, announcement or promo visual |
| 1 | Interactive story | 5 minutes max; the audience makes decisions and explores the consequences |
